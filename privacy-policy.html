TEMPOYA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TEMPOYA ("we", "us", or "our") is a social meeting platform designed for adults aged 18 and above. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use TEMPOYA, we may collect:

  • Name and profile information
  • Phone number and account information
  • Date of birth or age verification information
  • Profile photographs
  • Identity verification information
  • Selfie or liveness verification information
  • Language, interests and profile preferences
  • Approximate location information for discovery and meeting features
  • Chat and communication information within TEMPOYA
  • Meeting, booking and tariff information
  • Payment, refund and transaction information
  • Ratings, reports and safety-related information
  • Device and technical information needed to operate the service

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your TEMPOYA account
  • Verify users and help prevent fake accounts
  • Provide profile discovery and matching features
  • Enable mutual meeting requests and bookings
  • Process payments, refunds and settlements
  • Provide safety and emergency features
  • Detect fraud, scams, abuse and suspicious activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the security and performance of TEMPOYA
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

3. Location Information

TEMPOYA may use approximate location information to help users discover people within a selected distance range.

TEMPOYA is designed to avoid publicly displaying a user's exact home, workplace or other private location to other users.

Location sharing for safety purposes may be optional and subject to the user's settings and consent.

4. Identity Verification

TEMPOYA may use phone verification, age verification, identity verification and selfie/liveness verification to improve platform trust and safety.

Verification information may be processed by approved service providers where required. Sensitive verification information is not intended to be publicly displayed on user profiles.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. TEMPOYA does not intend to store full card numbers, CVV numbers or payment authentication credentials on its own systems.

Payment information may be processed according to the privacy policy and terms of the relevant payment provider.

6. Communication and Meetings

TEMPOYA may process information related to in-app communication, meeting requests, bookings, meeting duration, meeting completion, ratings and reports in order to operate the service and protect users.

7. Safety and Emergency Features

Users may be offered optional safety features such as emergency contacts, safety check-ins, location sharing during a safety session, reporting and blocking.

TEMPOYA is not a replacement for local emergency services. In an emergency, users should contact the appropriate local emergency service.

8. Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate TEMPOYA, such as hosting providers, verification providers, payment providers, analytics providers, customer support providers and security services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, court order or lawful government request.

9. User Control

Depending on applicable law and platform functionality, users may be able to access, update, correct or request deletion of certain personal information.

Users can also manage available privacy, location, communication and notification settings through the service.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

TEMPOYA is intended only for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly provide the service to children under 18.

12. International Users

TEMPOYA may be made available internationally. Personal information may therefore be processed or stored in countries other than the country where a user lives, subject to applicable privacy and data protection laws.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When important changes are made, we may provide an appropriate notice through the website or application.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact TEMPOYA through the official support channel provided on our website or application.

Important: This Privacy Policy is a website draft prepared for TEMPOYA's development stage. Before commercial launch, it should be reviewed and finalized for the countries where TEMPOYA operates, including applicable privacy, consumer protection, payment and data protection requirements.

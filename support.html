TEMPOYA Support

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to TEMPOYA Support. We are here to help with account, verification, meetings, payments, safety and other platform related questions.

How Can We Help?

Account & Verification

Get help with account access, phone verification, age verification, identity verification and profile-related issues.

Meetings & Matching

Get help with interests, mutual matches, meeting requests, meeting duration, venue selection and meeting completion.

Payments

Get help with payment status, protected payments, extensions, refunds, cancellations and payout-related questions.

Safety

If you experience harassment, threats, suspicious behaviour, scams or other safety concerns, please use the available report and blocking features.

In an immediate emergency, contact your local emergency services. TEMPOYA is not a replacement for emergency services.

Report a User

You can report fake profiles, harassment, scams, unsafe behaviour, misleading information, no-shows or other violations through the platform's reporting features.

Before Contacting Support

Please provide enough information for us to understand the issue. Never send your password, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or other confidential authentication information to anyone claiming to be TEMPOYA Support.

Important Safety Notice

TEMPOYA Support will never ask you for your password, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or private authentication credentials.

Contact

Official support contact details will be published here before commercial launch.

Please use only official TEMPOYA support channels listed on this website.

Response Times

Response times may vary depending on the type and urgency of the request. Safety-related reports may receive priority.

Account & Data Requests

Users may request assistance regarding account access, personal information, privacy requests and account-related issues through the official support process.

Remember: Never share passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or CVVs with anyone.

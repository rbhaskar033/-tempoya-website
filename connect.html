1. Publish your discovery profile

Use your profile name, age, country and language. Publishing allows other signed-in users to discover limited profile details. Do not add your phone number to your display name.

2. Discover & mutually match

Like sends an interest request. A match is created only if both users like each other. Block and report are available on each profile.

Chat is available only after a mutual match. Keep conversations respectful. Do not share financial details or sensitive personal data.

4. Booking a public meeting

Booking requests do not charge money. Both people must agree to a public venue and time. Payments are not enabled here.

Safety

Report or block users from their profile card. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Automated SOS, live location sharing and emergency-contact alerts are not enabled in this build.

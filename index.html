QUESTIONS

FAQ

Who is TEMPOYA for?

TEMPOYA is intended only for adults aged 18 and above.

Does payment guarantee a meeting or consent?

No. Payment never guarantees consent, personal access or physical interaction.

Are bookings and payments available now?

This website is an informational preview. Do not assume that app, matching, booking, verification, payment or SOS features are live until their official release is announced.

Can users choose a meeting place?

The planned service is designed around mutually agreed, suitable public venues. Feature availability will be confirmed at launch.

How can I report a safety concern?

Please use local emergency services if you are in immediate danger. Official TEMPOYA support and reporting channels must be published before user onboarding begins.

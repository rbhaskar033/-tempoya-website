QUESTIONS

FAQ

Who can use TEMPOYA?

TEMPOYA is intended for adults aged 18 and above.

Does payment guarantee a meeting or consent?

No. Payment does not guarantee consent, personal access or physical interaction.

Can users choose the meeting place?

Yes. Users can suggest suitable public cafés or restaurants and both users must agree.

Can users continue a meeting?

Yes. Both users must agree to an extension and complete the additional payment before the timer is extended.

Can users report or block someone?

Yes. Users can report unsafe behaviour, scams, fake profiles, harassment, no-shows and other issues.
